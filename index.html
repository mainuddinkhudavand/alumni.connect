📖 Help Center & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about AlumniConnect

🎓 How do I update my profile and job status?
Navigate to the Profile tab in the bottom bar or tap your avatar at the top. You can update your company, current status (Employed, Studying, etc.), CGPA, LinkedIn, and GitHub profile URL.
💼 How do I apply for opportunities in the Hub?
Open the Opportunities Hub from the bottom navigation. Click any vacancy card to read eligibility, salary/stipend, and deadline details. Click "Apply Now" to open the direct application link.
💬 Can I message alumni and coordinators directly?
Yes! Tap on any alumni or student in the Directory to view their card and tap "Send Direct Message", or tap "✍️ New Chat" in the Messages tab.
🔒 How does 2-Factor Authentication protect my account?
Enabling 2FA in Settings enforces an additional email code verification handshake whenever a login occurs from a new session or browser.
🖼️ What image formats and sizes are supported?
You can upload JPG, PNG, or WEBP photos up to 150MB. Images are automatically optimized for fast, crystal-clear loading.
